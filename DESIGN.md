# Jugnu's Saloon - Design System & Guidelines (DESIGN.md)

> **MANDATORY DESIGN RULE**: Whenever processing queries, adding features, or modifying UI for Jugnu's Saloon, you MUST consult and adhere strictly to this `DESIGN.md`. All components, color schemes, typography, layout structures, and visual elements must maintain the clean light off-white, obsidian black, and gold aesthetic defined here.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name**: Jugnu's Saloon (Logo: Monogram "JS" in Gold Crest)
- **Scope**: Full-Service Beauty Saloon, Spa & Bridal Lounge.
- **Aesthetic**: Clean, Ultra-Professional, High-End Modern Luxury. Crisp Off-White backdrop (`#FAFAFA`), Obsidian Black headings & buttons (`#111111`), subtle Gold glares/accents (`#D4AF37`), and Helvetica typography.

---

## 2. Layout & Container Width
- **Wide Canvas Max-Width**: `max-w-[1480px]` across all sections to provide generous wide-screen layout without empty margins on left or right.

---

## 3. STRICT DESIGN REGULATION: NO PILL TAGS / BADGES OVER HEADINGS
- **DO NOT** place pill tags, mini badges, or category labels above section titles.
- All headings must be direct, clean, bold, and un-cluttered.

---

## 4. Color Tokens & Typography
- **Primary Canvas**: `#FAFAFA` (Crisp Off-White main background)
- **Section White**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White card & section panels)
- **Text & Headings**: `#111111` (Deep Obsidian Black)
- **Accent Gold**: `#D4AF37` / `#996515`
- **Font Stack**: `"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`
